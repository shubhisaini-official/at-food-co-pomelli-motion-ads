## 📐 2. Creative Hook Engineering Principles

* **The 3-Second Rule:** The initial frame must feature high-contrast visual movement (fresh ingredients being plated) paired with large, legible serif headlines.
* **Subhead Text Animation:** Staggering headline and subhead entrance times ensures viewers digest the core value proposition before the call-to-action appears.
* **Color Hierarchy in Motion:** Applying Forest Green (`#2E7D32`) background banners ensures instant brand recognition across social feeds.

---

## ⚡ 3. Real-World Execution Critique

* **Pros:** Generates short-form video animations complete with kinetic text overlays in under 60 seconds, eliminating manual editing in Premiere or CapCut.
* **Limitations:** Motion trajectories are template-based; hyper-custom transitions require exporting individual assets into dedicated video editing software.
